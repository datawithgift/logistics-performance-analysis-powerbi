# Logistics Performance Analysis & Interactive Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze logistics operations and business performance. The interactive dashboard provides insights into revenue, profitability, delivery performance, driver safety, customer contributions, and fleet operations.

The goal is to transform logistics data into meaningful insights that can help management evaluate operational performance, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions.

## Project Objectives
Monitor key logistics and financial performance indicators.

Identify trends in revenue, deliveries, and operating costs.

Examine driver productivity and safety performance.

Understand customer revenue contributions.

Evaluate fleet utilisation and fuel efficiency.

Develop actionable recommendations based on data analysis.

## Tools Used
Microsoft Power BI: Data visualization, dashboard development, and performance monitoring.

Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation.

DAX: KPI calculations and analytical measures.

## Key Findings
The dashboard highlights several areas that deserve management attention:

Delivery performance: Approximately 55.7% of deliveries are on time, while 44.3% are late, indicating an opportunity to investigate and reduce delivery delays.

Operational costs: Fuel represents approximately 91.9% of the costs shown in the operational cost breakdown, making fuel efficiency an important area to investigate.

Revenue and profitability: The dashboard reports $298.6 million in revenue and $194.6 million in profit. These figures provide a starting point for evaluating financial performance.

Revenue trends: Monthly revenue fluctuates throughout the year, suggesting a need to investigate changes in demand, delivery volumes, and customer activity.

Driver safety: Monthly incident counts vary, highlighting the importance of monitoring safety performance and identifying recurring incident patterns.

Fleet efficiency: Differences in truck utilisation and fuel expenditure create opportunities to investigate vehicle performance and operating costs.

## Business Recommendations
Based on the initial dashboard analysis, the following actions could help improve logistics performance:

Improve delivery reliability: Investigate late deliveries by route, driver, and month to identify recurring causes of delays.

Control fuel expenditure: Compare fuel consumption against distance travelled and completed trips to identify opportunities to improve fuel efficiency.

Strengthen driver safety: Review incident patterns and provide targeted training or operational interventions where appropriate.

Monitor customer performance: Evaluate major customers by revenue, profitability, and service requirements to support customer retention and resource planning.

Optimise fleet utilisation: Compare truck utilisation, maintenance expenditure, and delivery output to identify potential fleet allocation improvements.

Monitor business trends: Track monthly revenue, delivery volumes, and operating costs to support planning and performance management.

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to bring financial, customer, delivery, driver, and fleet data together in an interactive reporting environment.

By highlighting delivery delays, fuel expenditure, safety incidents, and variations in business performance, the dashboard provides a foundation for further investigation and data-driven operational improvements.
